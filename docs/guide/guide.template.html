Chapter 1

The three parts, and how they fit

There are three things, not one. Knowing which is which saves most of the confusion.

Part one

The office software

Where your staff work. Admissions, attendance, fees, exams, reports. Opens in a web browser. Also available to install on a Windows computer.

Part two

The parent portal

The same software, but a parent signing in sees only their own children: what they owe, whether they came to school, and their result.

Part three

The public website

Where a new school reads the prices, starts a free trial, and downloads the Windows installer.

All three read the same records. When your clerk takes a fee at the counter, the parent's portal shows it paid straight away. There is no copying between them and no "syncing" to remember.

Worth knowing

Your school's records are yours alone. Another school using this same software cannot see one line of them, and neither can a parent from another family. That is enforced by the database itself, not by being careful.

Chapter 2

Getting in

One email address and one password for each person. Never share a login: every entry is stamped with who made it, and a shared login makes that stamp worthless.

  1. Open the address your school was given. It looks like app.yourschool.pk.

  2. Type your email and your password.

  3. Press Sign in.

    You land on the dashboard, or on your own class if you are a teacher.

If you have forgotten your password

  1. Press Forgotten your password?

  2. Type the email address you sign in with. Press Send the link.

  3. Open your email. Look in the spam or junk folder as well, because reset emails often land there.

  4. Press the link in the email. Choose a new password and type it twice.

    The link works once and lasts one hour. After that, ask for a new one.

Changing your own password

At the bottom of the parent portal, or on the left in the office software, press Password or Change your password. You must type your current password first. That is what stops somebody walking past an unattended computer and locking you out.

Careful

You will be signed out after changing it, and asked to sign in again with the new one. That is on purpose: it proves the new password works before you walk away from the computer.

Chapter 3

The first day: setting up

Do these in order. Each one needs the one before it. Skipping ahead is the single commonest reason a new school gets stuck.

  1. Your school's details. Settings → School Profile. Name, address, phone. Upload your logo here too, and it then prints on every receipt, challan, result card and certificate.

  2. The academic session. Settings → Sessions. Add this year, for example 2026-2027, and mark it current.

    Nothing else works until one session is current. Every child, fee and exam belongs to a session.

  3. Classes and sections. Settings → Classes & Sections. Add each class in order, lowest first. Add sections only if you have them.

  4. Fee heads. Settings → Fee Heads. A fee head is a kind of charge: Tuition, Admission, Transport, Security deposit. Mark the ones that repeat every month as recurring.

  5. Fee structure. Settings → Fee Structure. Now put an amount against each class and each head. This is the grid that decides what a challan says.

  6. Your staff. Staff → Staff. Add each person. Give a login only to those who need one.

The mistake to avoid

If a class has children but no fee amounts, generating challans produces Rs 0 slips and reports success. The school looks billed when it is not. The dashboard warns you about this by name. Do not ignore that yellow strip.

Which year am I working in?

Look at the grey chip on the bar across the top of every screen, just to the left of where it names the page you are on. It says 2026-2027 or whatever your current session is called. Everything you type is recorded against that session, and there is no year selector anywhere: the software always uses the one marked current.

If that chip turns amber and says "ended", the session's last day has passed and you are still entering into it. That is the moment to go to Settings → Sessions and mark the new year current. A whole term of attendance and marks has been filed under the wrong year in schools that missed this.

Chapter 4

Adding your children

One at a time for a new admission. In bulk when you are moving from paper or from another system.

One new admission

  1. Admissions → fill the form. Name, father's name, father's CNIC, class, and a GR number.

  2. If the child has a brother or sister already in the school, search for them in Siblings and add the link.

  3. Press Admit.

    The child gets a GR number, an enrolment in the class, and a family. From now on the software knows who to bill.

Why the sibling link matters

Linking brothers and sisters into one family means the father pays once for all his children: one amount, one receipt, one entry in the day book. Without it he queues three times.

Missed it at admission? Open the child's profile and press + Link a sibling or relative, then Put in one family on the row it adds.

Many children at once

  1. Settings → Import. Download the sample file first.

  2. Fill your children into that file, keeping the column headings exactly as they are.

  3. Upload it. The software shows you what it would do before it does anything.

  4. Read that list. If it is right, press the button to import.

    Rows with a problem are named with the reason. Fix them in the file and upload again, and nothing is half-done.

Chapter 5

Your staff, and who teaches what

Two separate things, and both matter for exams.

Class teachers

Staff → Class Teachers. Pick a class, then choose the teacher for each section. A class teacher can mark attendance for their class and enter marks for any subject in it.

Subject teachers

Staff → Subject Teachers. Pick a class, then tap the teachers who teach each subject.

This decides who can change a result

A teacher may enter marks only for a class they are class teacher of, or a class and subject listed here. Everybody else is refused, including for the exam marks that go on the result card.

A subject with nobody assigned can only be marked by the office. The screen counts those for you, so fill them in before exam week and not during it.

Chapter 6

Every morning: attendance

A minute per class. It feeds the parent portal, the monthly register, and the attendance line on every result card.

  1. Attendance. Choose your class and section. Today's date is already filled in.

  2. Everybody starts as present. Change only the children who are not.

  3. Press Save.

The five marks are present, absent, late, half day and leave. Late and half day still count as attending; leave and absent do not.

Correcting a mistake

You can change today's marks. Changing an older day is recorded, and the principal can see every such change under Reports → Attendance Changes. That is protection for you as much as oversight of you: it proves what you entered and when.

Finalising, and telling the parents

When you are sure, press Finalise. Two things happen. The day is locked, so nobody can change it quietly. And a WhatsApp message is prepared for each absent child, waiting under Messages, naming that one child and that one date.

Nothing is sent without you

The messages sit in a list. You press the WhatsApp button on each one and send it yourself. Children marked leave or half day get nothing: a parent who told you their child would be away does not need telling back.

Finalise the same day twice and you do not get two messages. If you corrected the register and finalised again, only the children you had not already messaged are added.

If you do not want absence messages at all, switch them off: Settings → Messages → Absent today. The register still finalises.

Chapter 7

Every day: taking fees

The busiest screen in the building. Designed for fifteen seconds per parent.

  1. Fees → Collect a fee.

  2. Find the payer. Any of these work: the father's CNIC, his phone number, the child's name, the GR number, or the code on a printed challan.

  3. Look at the family sheet. Every child is listed with what each owes.

  4. Type the amount received and choose how it was paid.

  5. Press Receive.

    One receipt number, for the whole family.

  6. Press Print receipt and hand it over.

How the money is split

Oldest month first, across all the children in the family. So a father paying part of what he owes clears the oldest challans, not the newest. If he pays more than he owes, the extra is held as advance and comes off next month's challan automatically.

Cheques and bank transfers

Choose pending for money that has not cleared yet. A pending payment does not reduce what the family owes and does not print a receipt. When the money appears in the bank, mark it verified under Fees → Pending clearances, and the receipt is issued then.

This is why: a receipt for a cheque that later bounces is a piece of paper you cannot take back.

Cash at the end of the day

Fees → Till. Count the cash drawer and enter what is actually in it. The software shows what it expected. Any difference is recorded with your name against it, which protects the honest clerk, because the day it balances is on record too.

Chapter 8

Every month: challans and chasing dues

Bill everybody once, then work a list instead of guessing.

Raising the month's challans

  1. Fees → Generate challans. Choose the month and the classes.

  2. Read the summary it shows you before anything is created.

  3. Confirm. Every child now has a challan for that month.

  4. Print them class by class and send them home.

Stop if you see Rs 0

A challan for Rs 0 means that class has no fee amounts set. Do not send it. Go to Settings → Fee Structure, fill the amounts in, then generate again.

Who has not paid

Reports → Defaulters gives you the list, with how many days overdue and a phone number for each. Reports → Dues by Fee Head answers a different question: which charge is not being paid. If almost all of your Rs 386,000 is the transport fee, that is a different problem from eighty families behind on tuition.

Reminding parents on WhatsApp

  1. Messages. The list is already written for you, one row per family.

  2. Press the WhatsApp button on a row. WhatsApp opens with the message typed out.

  3. Read it, then send it yourself.

Nothing is ever sent without you pressing send. You can edit the wording of every message under Settings → Messages, and switch any of them off.

A challan you should not have raised

It happens: the wrong month, the wrong class, or a child who had already left. The fix is Cancel charge, and only the owner or principal can do it.

  1. Open the child's profile → Fees.

  2. Find the month. Press Cancel charge.

  3. Type why. A few words at least, because somebody reads this months later.

The challan stops counting: it leaves the family's balance, the defaulters list, the unpaid-challan report and the parent portal, and it will no longer print. It is not deleted. Every cancellation is listed under Reports → Cancelled charges with the amount, your name and your reason, and a clerk can read that list even though they cannot cancel anything.

Cancel and Delay are opposites

Delay says you still owe this, later. The reminder pauses, the money is still due. Cancel says you never owed it. Reaching for the wrong one either leaves money uncollected or chases a family for something they never owed.

If the family has already paid

You cannot cancel a challan with money against it, and the software will tell you the amount. Reverse the payment first, which writes a matching reversal receipt, so the cash movement stays visible, and then cancel the charge. Doing it the other way round would leave a receipt pointing at a charge that no longer exists.

Raising fees for next year

Settings → Fee Increase. Choose the session, a percentage or a flat amount, and the date it starts. Press Preview the change and read the list, which shows every fee going from what to what. Only then press Apply.

Challans already sent are safe

An increase dated 1 April does not change a challan raised in March. The old challan keeps the old amount. Nothing you have already given a parent is rewritten.

Chapter 9

Money going out

Record what you spend and the software can tell you what the school actually kept.

  1. Accounts. Type the amount and choose a category: Salaries, Rent, Utilities, and so on.

  2. Fill in who it was paid to, and how.

  3. Press Record expense. You get a voucher number.

Salaries go here too. That is what keeps the profit figure honest without a separate payroll system.

Your own categories

Eight are provided. If your real costs include generator diesel or a security guard, press Manage categories and add them. Filing everything under "Other" makes the expense report answer nothing.

You can retire a category but never delete one, because old vouchers keep it, so last year's reports still add up.

The day book

Reports → Day Book / Cash. Everything in and out for a day, in order, with who recorded it. This is the page a proprietor asks for.

Chapter 10

Exam time

Set up the papers, enter the marks, then release the results deliberately.

  1. The term. Exams → Terms. Add "First Term" or "Annual".

  2. The papers. For each class, add each subject with its total marks, its pass marks, and the practical total if it has one.

  3. The marks. Each teacher opens their own class and subject and types the marks. Absent is a tick, not a zero.

  4. Generate the cards. The software works out totals, percentage, grade, position and pass or fail.

  5. Check them. Read a few before anybody else sees them.

  6. Release. Only now do parents see anything.

Generating is not releasing

You can generate cards as often as you like: to check them, or after a correction. Parents see nothing until you press release. That is deliberate: a mark a parent saw and then saw change turns every correction into an accusation.

Releasing also prepares a WhatsApp message for each family, waiting under Messages, telling them the result can be viewed. One message per child per term. Release again after a correction and nobody is messaged twice. A result you are withholding over unpaid fees is never announced, because that family cannot see it yet.

Letters or a GPA

Settings → School profile → Grade scale chooses between Letter (A+, A, B, C, D, E, F) and GPA (10-point). Set it before you generate a term's cards.

On the GPA scale each paper gets a grade point out of 10, and the figure at the top of the card is the average of those points, shown as GPA rather than Grade. That is not the same as banding the overall percentage, and the difference is the point: two children can both total 280 out of 400 and get 8.0 and 7.5, because one was steady across four papers and the other was brilliant in two and weak in two.

Cards already printed do not change

A card carries the scale it was made with. Switch to GPA in March and the cards you printed in December still say Grade A when you reprint them, so a reprint always matches the paper the family is holding.

Marks that are not final

A card generated while some papers are unmarked is provisional, and says so on its face and in the portal. Its percentage covers only the papers that have been marked.

Explain this before a parent phones

A child can have a good percentage and still read Not passed, because one paper is below the pass mark. That is how most Pakistani schools grade, and the card is doing what your own rules say. The failed subject is marked in red in the list, so you can point at it.

Chapter 11

Certificates and leaving

A school leaving certificate is a legal document. The software numbers them so you can answer for every one.

  1. Open the child's profile. Press School Leaving Certificate or Character Certificate.

  2. Check the details and print it. It takes the next serial number in your register.

  3. Record the leaving with a date and a reason.

Every certificate ever issued is listed under Reports → Certificates: which serial, to whom, on what date, whether it was a duplicate, and whether it was released while fees were still owed.

A child who has left

They stop counting in your student numbers and stop being billed, but their whole history stays: fees, attendance, results. You can still print their certificate next year when they come back asking for it.

Chapter 12

Parents: the portal

Give a family a login and they stop telephoning the office to ask what they owe.

  1. Open any child of the family. Find Parent access.

  2. Type the father's or mother's email address and create the login.

  3. Tell them the address of the software and that they can sign in.

    One login covers every child in that family.

A parent can see, for each of their children, what is owed and what has been paid; whether the child came to school each day; and the result, once you have released it. They can also print a statement of their own account.

What a parent can never see

Another family's children. Any list of your staff. Any of your money records beyond their own bill. This is not a setting you can get wrong, because the database refuses those reads for a parent account.

Chapter 14

Your own bill for the software

What you owe us, where to pay it, and how to prove you paid. Owner and principal only.

Settings → Subscription. It shows your plan, the date it runs to, how many students you have against your limit, and every invoice we have issued you.

  1. Open the invoice and print it. It carries our NTN, so your accountant can claim it.

  2. Pay to the bank account printed on it.

  3. Come back and press I have paid. Enter the amount, the date and the transaction reference.

  4. We confirm it, and your dates extend.

If the subscription lapses

You get a grace period first, with a warning in the software. After that the software locks: you can still read and print everything, but nothing new can be entered. Your records are never deleted for non-payment.

Chapter 15

When something goes wrong

Read the message. It names the problem and usually the screen that fixes it.

What you seeWhat it means, and what to do
Challans come out Rs 0 That class has no fee amounts. Settings → Fee Structure.
"You can only enter marks for a class and subject you teach" You are not listed for it. Ask the office: Staff → Subject Teachers.
A parent signs in and sees "No children linked yet" The login was made but not attached to a family. Redo Parent access from the child's profile.
A father pays once but two children still show dues They are not in one family. Open a profile → link the sibling → Put in one family.
The printed page comes out blank Print from the button on the document itself, not your browser's menu.
"Your subscription has expired" Settings → Subscription. Reading and printing still work.
A number looks wrong Do not correct it by re-entering. Find the entry, in Reports → Student Ledger or Day Book, and reverse it, so both the mistake and the fix are on record.
Nothing you do is unrecoverable

Payments are reversed, not deleted. Certificates are cancelled, not removed. Marks keep what they were before a change. Every one of those leaves a record with your name and the reason, which is exactly what you want when somebody asks a question six months later.

The observer role

An observer can read everything and change nothing. Use it for an auditor, a board member, or a new member of staff still learning. They cannot break anything, and the software will not pretend otherwise by showing them empty screens.
