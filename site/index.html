How it works

Three screens your office will actually live in

Everything else is reporting. These are the ones that run all day.

Take the fee in fifteen seconds

Search by the father's CNIC, a phone number, or any child's name. Every child in the family comes up together. Enter one amount. It clears the oldest month first, across all of them, and prints one receipt.

Mark the register from a phone

Teachers open the class list, tap the absentees, and finalise. It keeps working when the internet drops: marks queue on the phone and sync the moment the connection returns.

See where the month went

Fees collected, expenses paid, profit for the month. Which classes are behind. Which families to ring. Who took which payment, and whether the cash drawer balanced at four o'clock.

Fee control

Know what should have been collected

Most school software tells you what was collected. That number is only useful if you already trust it. The harder and more valuable question is what the roll says should have arrived, and where the difference went.

Every receipt number is issued in an unbroken sequence. Nothing is ever deleted. A mistake is corrected by a reversal that leaves both entries in the record, with a reason and a name attached.

  • Expected against collected, class by class, from the real roll.
  • Ghost student check: anyone being billed who never appears in a register.
  • Discounts need approval. A clerk proposes, the principal approves, both are recorded.
  • Cash drawer settlement: each collector counts their own drawer, and a difference needs a written reason.
  • Full audit trail: every change to money, marks and attendance, with who and when.

Questions

Questions a principal actually asks

What happens to our data if we stop paying?

It stays yours. Export everything at any time, including after you have cancelled, in a plain readable format. We do not hold records to ransom and there is no charge for taking your own data out.

Does it work when the internet goes down?

Attendance does. Teachers can keep marking the register and it syncs when the connection returns. Fee collection needs the connection, because two clerks taking cash offline would issue two receipts with the same number, and a fee book with duplicate numbers is worse than a fee book you have to wait five minutes for.

Can a clerk change a receipt after it is printed?

No. Nothing is deleted or edited. A mistake is corrected by a reversal entry, so both lines stay in the ledger with a reason and the name of the person who made the correction. Receipt numbers are issued in an unbroken sequence, so a missing number is itself visible.

Can parents see other children's information?

A parent login reaches only that family's own children: their fee balance, their receipts, their attendance and their results once you publish them. It cannot reach any other family, and it cannot reach anything you have not published.

We already have years of records in Excel. Can we move them?

Yes. Students, staff and opening balances import from a spreadsheet. Bring us the file you already keep and we will map it with you rather than asking you to retype a school.

What about results that are not final yet?

Marks stay unpublished until you publish them, and a result card can be withheld for a single student, for example while a fee matter is settled. Parents see nothing until you decide they should.

Is it in Urdu?

The screens are in English. Anything you type is accepted and printed in Urdu, including names, addresses and remarks, so a certificate or a fee receipt can carry Urdu text. A full Urdu interface is not built yet, and we would rather say so than imply otherwise.
